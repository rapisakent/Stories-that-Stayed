Editor's note

What these stories taught me

These stories taught me that life isn't always about having everything figured out. Sometimes we lose people, make mistakes, experience unexpected changes, and question ourselves. But those experiences don't mean our story is over.

I've learned that growth also means accepting the things we cannot control, taking responsibility for our actions, and learning to become a better version of ourselves.

I didn't choose these three because they're popular. I chose them because each one met me in a different part of growing up.

— Kent Rapisa

Not everything needs a perfect ending to have meaning.
